Education

Lodha Samaj Indore intends to offer inspiration and help to the children of its community with their studies, their careers and their vocational training.

What the Community Wishes to Do

  • Inspiration and help with children's studies
  • Guidance about careers
  • Inspiration and help to take vocational training and similar courses

Swami Brahmanand Lodhi (स्वामी ब्रह्मानंद लोधी जी) called on the people of the community to turn their attention to education — read about him on the About Us page.

An Invitation to Capable Community Members

Capable and well-placed Lodhi brothers who wish to offer such service work through Lodha Samaj are warmly invited. Please speak to us on the phone number above or through the contact page.
