What to Ask Where

There is one phone number, but a look here first often saves the call:

  • Giving to a bhandara or a yatra — what can be given, and who has given, is on the donate page
  • A name or spelling in a donor list — if a name is printed wrongly, left out, or written two different ways, tell us on the phone or by email; the list is corrected
  • Dates and times of events — on the events page
  • Registering a family — on the register page
  • Education or health — the education and health pages carry the government portals
  • Sending a photograph — see the gallery page

In an emergency do not wait for the community's phone — call 112, or 108 for an ambulance.
