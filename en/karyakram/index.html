Vishal Bhandara

Every year during Navratri, Lodha Samaj Indore holds the Vishal Bhandara (a large community meal) and the Nagar Kanya Pujan. The event is possible only with the support of the community's families.

Support for the bhandara can be given as sugar, flour, oil, salt, ghee, mawa or cash. The names of those who contribute are recorded in the donor list.
