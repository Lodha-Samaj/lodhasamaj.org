Register

Registration is the way to join the community — to have your family's details recorded with the community, so that the community can be organised and its members can stay in touch with one another.

What Registering Does

  • News of the community's events reaches you in time
  • If someone needs blood, the समाज can call the right person — see the health page
  • News about scholarships and studies reaches the families whose children are studying — see the education page
  • Families can reach each other, and the समाज knows its own numbers

What to Have Ready

Registering on the phone or at the dharmshala is done in one go if you can say:

  • The name of the head of the family and the names of those at home
  • An address and a phone number that news can be sent to
  • Who at home is studying, and in which class or course
  • Blood group, if it is known
  • The village or place the family came to Indore from

Your Information

This website collects nothing — there is no form on it and no account to make. Registration today happens on the phone or at the dharmshala, and what is said stays in the community's register.

What the site shows publicly is only the donors' names and what they gave, which the समाज has always published in its own notices. Nobody's phone number or address is here.

The Online Form

Registration will also be possible through an online form. When the form is ready, its link will appear on this page.
