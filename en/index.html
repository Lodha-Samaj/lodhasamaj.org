The tradition of a brave woman, a resolve to serve

Rani Avanti Bai Lodhi (रानी अवंती बाई लोधी) was the first woman martyr and brave heroine to play an important part in India's first War of Independence, in 1857.

Born on 16 August 1831 in the village of Mankehni, Seoni district, the Rani took up the rule of the Ramgarh state, raised an army of four thousand soldiers and, in 1857, defeated the British force at the village of Khairi near Mandla.

On 20 March 1858, surrounded on all sides, she gave up her life by her own sword rather than fall into British hands. She was only 26 years old.

Our Purpose

The purpose of Lodha Samaj Indore is to bring service, help and guidance to every member of the community. Lodha Samaj Indore has always favoured adopting the latest technology for the progress of its members. Through this web portal, service work will be undertaken to inspire and help children with their studies, their careers and vocational training, and to provide help when needed through medical facilities and a blood bank. Capable and well-placed Lodhi brothers who wish to take part in this service work are warmly invited.
