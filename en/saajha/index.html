In a paragraph

The website of Lodha Samaj Indore is now ready. It carries the dates and times of
the Navratri events, year-by-year donor lists for all three events — who gave what,
with names and quantities — information on scholarships and government health
services, the office bearers, photographs of the events, and how to reach the
community. All in one place, in both Hindi and English, and just as easy to use on
a phone. — lodhasamaj.org

One thing to keep in mind

The donor lists show only a name and what was given — no phone number, no address, nothing else. Even so, if someone would rather their name were not there, say so and it will be taken down.
